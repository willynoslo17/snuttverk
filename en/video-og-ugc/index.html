Video and UGC

Short clips that fit in the mobile feed - made with real people from your company.

What is a short video?

A short video is vertical (9:16), typically 15–45 seconds, with embedded subtitles. Most people watch without sound, so the text has to work on its own.

What is UGC style?

UGC style is authentic video taken with mobile. It's owner, employees or real customers with written consent - not studio set-up with a script that feels foreign.

No actors pretending to be customers, no fake reviews, and AI generated people are not used as "customers".
