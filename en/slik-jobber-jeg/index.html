This is how I work

A clear process without surprises. The schedule is agreed in the offer.

  1. 1. Free call 30 minutes – video or in person in Oslo. I talk to you about the company, the channels and what you need.
  2. 2. Fixed offer Written scope and price before commencement.
  3. 3. Startup Review of the profiles, tone and content plan for the first month.
  4. 4. Recording and production Recording from you or at your place in the Oslo area, then editing and text.
  5. 5. You approve Nothing goes out without your OK.
  6. 6. Publication The content is published in agreed channels.
  7. 7. Monthly report Reach, impressions and clicks as the platforms show them - without promises of results.
