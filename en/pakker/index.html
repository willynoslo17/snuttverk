Frequently asked questions

What do you need from me?

Access to the channels (as partner/role, not password), logo and profile material, raw clips or recordings, and approximately 30 minutes for approval per month.

Who owns the content?

You get the right to use all posts and videos in your own channels and paid advertisements, without time limit. Music and stock material follow a separate license.

Do I have to approve everything?

Yes. Nothing is published without your approval.

How do you access the accounts?

Via partner roles or accesses in each platform, for example Meta Business Suite. I never ask for your password.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for ideas, text drafts, subtitling and translation. The people featured are real, and realistic AI-generated content is branded as such.
